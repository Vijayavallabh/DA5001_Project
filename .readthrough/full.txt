011                                              Abstract
012
013             Inference-time mechanisms for copyright protection meter how far a lan-
014             guage model’s output may drift from a safe model that never saw the pro-
015             tected work, and publish the allowance as a single number k. We show
016             that k carries no risk information on its own, and identify what does. The
017             bound such a budget implies on reproducing a work x becomes vacuous at
018
                K = S(x), the safe model’s surprisal of x, and this threshold is identical
                for every Rényi order of the charge, so no strengthening of the accounting
019
                moves it. A second result prices the other thing a budget buys: Donsker–
020
                Varadhan at the sequence level bounds the budget needed for mean utility
021             by the Cramér rate function of that utility under the safe model. Measuring
022             both sides, the decoder spends 79 to 171 nats of realised divergence for a
023             utility gain worth hundredths of a nat — three to four orders of magnitude
024             of overhead, so what pushes a budget past the vacuity threshold is the price
025             of imitating the risky model, not the price of being better than the safe one.
026             The useful region opens only where the certified one has closed: under two
027             independent judges and 600 comparisons per budget, neither separates the
028             decoder from its own safe model at k = 0.6, barely past the budget at which
029
                the first protected work loses its certificate, and both separate it only at
                k ≥ 10, where none of them is covered. The two boundaries are ordered
030
                rather than coincident: the sharper judge first sees a difference at k = 0.66,
031
                after the certificate has begun to fail. Finally, extraction begins near that
032             threshold: across seven model pairs with seven distinct anchors and five
033             tokenizers, leakage first becomes measurable between 0.88 and 1.17 of s(x),
034             and between 0.878 and 0.926 among the five whose adversary holds a com-
035             parable amount of the work — a quantity extraction evaluations specify in
036             tokens, and therefore report differently under every tokenizer. Halving it
037             moves a pair across its own vacuity threshold, and warping the anchor to
038             move s(x) within one pair moves the onset with it, at an elasticity of +0.72
039             and +0.61: not what a budget in a fixed number of nats would give. Across
040
                ten safe models and three openly licensed corpora, the separation between
                the rate that must be permitted and the rate that must be forbidden widens
041
                as safe models improve.

044   1     Introduction
045
046   A growing family of inference-time mechanisms promises to bound how much a language
047   model can reproduce a protected work by metering how far its output distribution may drift
048   from a safe model that never saw the work. The drift is charged in nats, accumulated in
049   a token bucket, and published as a single number k. Anchored decoding (He et al., 2026)
050
      is the current instance; the guarantee it enforces, k-near-access-freeness, goes back to Vyas
      et al. (2023). The appeal is that k looks like ε in differential privacy: a dial a deployer sets,
051
      a number a rights-holder can read.
052
053   This paper argues that k does not carry that meaning, and — more usefully — says exactly
      what does. Three results, in increasing order of what they cost to establish.

      The budget has a unit, and it is not nats. The bound a budget K implies on reproducing
055   a work x is vacuous once K reaches S(x), the safe model’s total surprisal of x, and the
056   threshold is the same for every Rényi order of the charge (Proposition 1). Raising the order
057   buys a tighter bound strictly below the threshold and does not move it, so the failure cannot
058   be repaired by strengthening the accounting. The quantity that carries risk is therefore
059   k/s(x), not k. Vyas et al. (2023) already report their budget as a fraction of the safe
060   model’s entropy; what is new is an exact threshold at the realised surprisal of a specific
061   work, holding for every order.
062   Almost none of what a budget buys is utility. A budget is one number, so whatever the
063   decoder buys with it comes from one account. Donsker–Varadhan at the sequence level gives
064   K ≥ Λ∗s (E[U ]), the Cramér rate function of the utility under the safe model (Theorem 1).
065   Both sides are measurable, and measuring them is what makes the result useful: the decoder
066   spends 79 to 171 nats of realised divergence to buy a utility gain an optimal policy could
067   buy for hundredths of a nat, a ratio above 103 at every budget. The cost of a metered
068   decoder is therefore approximation overhead rather than information-theoretic necessity —
069
      what drives a budget past s(x) is the price of imitating the risky model everywhere, not the
      price of being better. Empirically the useful region opens only where the certified one has
070
      closed. Two judges outside the audit, 600 comparisons per budget, agree at both ends and
071
      nowhere else: at k = 0.5, still inside the region where every protected passage is covered,
072   neither distinguishes the decoder from its own safe model (−1.03σ, +1.52σ); only at k ≥ 10,
073   where the certificate covers none, do both (−6.97σ, −3.29σ). Between those they disagree
074   about the crossing by nearly an order of magnitude in k, which is itself a result.
075
      Extraction begins near that threshold, and s(x) says where. Across seven pairs with seven
076
      distinct anchors and five tokenizers, leakage first becomes measurable between 0.88 and 1.17
077
      of s(x); among the five whose adversary is handed 13.0–14.4 words of the work it is 0.878 to
078   0.926, over nearly the whole range of s(x). What moves it is not the memoriser, the target’s
079   length or the vocabulary but that context, which extraction evaluations specify in tokens, so
080   a finer tokenizer hands the adversary half the words: pre-registered interventions changing
081   only that number move a pair across its own vacuity threshold in both directions. Warping
082   the anchor to move s(x) itself within a pair gives an elasticity of +0.72 and +0.61, refuting a
083   budget quoted in a fixed number of nats. The geometry’s own candidate, r(x) = ss (x)−sr (x)
084   (Eq. (4)), committed for five pairs before they were swept, is refuted with it.
085   Read together these say something a deployer can act on. The three rates that matter — the
086   surprisal rate s(x), the requirement r(x) a decoder must afford before it can pay for the work
087   at all, and the rate cuse at which ordinary traﬀic diverges — are properties of the models and
088   the content, computable before any mechanism is built. Measured across ten safe models
089   and three independently curated, openly licensed corpora, the separation between the rate
090   that must be permitted and the rate that must be forbidden widens as safe models improve
091   (Section 3), correcting a claim in an earlier audit of this mechanism (Vijayavallabh, 2026)
092
      that read one side of that two-sided quantity and inverted the trend.
093   None of this contradicts the mechanisms’ own theorems: across more than 100,000 budgeted
094   trajectories here and in the audits this builds on, no per-trajectory violation occurs. The
095   gap is between what the theorems bound and what the published number is taken to mean.

128   The proof (Appendix A) is three standard conversions — the α = ∞ change of measure,

137

138
139   The proof is in Appendix A; it needs only that the charge dominates the KL, which every
140   order α ≥ 1 does, so the boundary belongs to the family and not to one accounting rule.
141   Neither is kcrit our invention: it is the Loynes running maximum representing the workload of
142   a queue (Loynes, 1962), equivalently the smallest rate of a (σ, ρ) arrival curve admitting the
143   surprisal process (Cruz, 1991; Le Boudec & Thiran, 2001). Naming it makes the diagnosis
144
      concrete — a budget publishes a drift where safety is a workload maximum.
145   Together the two propositions bracket a regime. Below s(x) the certificate says some-
146   thing and the decoder is heavily constrained; above kcrit (x) the work is reproducible; on
147   [s(x), kcrit (x)) the decoder still protects the work and no budget in the family can say so,
148   the certificate reporting the drift of the surprisal process where safety is governed by its
149   running maximum. That is the distinction a token bucket makes between a mean rate and
150
      a burst, and it is why a scalar budget is the wrong primitive rather than merely a loose one.
151
152
      How wide the uncertified regime is, and who owns it Measured on 758 reference passages
      under four safe models from three openly licensed corpora, kcrit /s has a median of 4.25
153
      to 6.10, so the uncertified interval is wide — and almost entirely an artifact of where a
154
      generation starts. The running maximum is attained at the very first token in 87.7% to
155   90.5% of cases, because the bucket must pay for the opening out of one step’s allowance,
156   and an adversary supplying a single genuine prefix token collapses the ratio to 1.32–1.48,
157   where it stays for 5, 10 or 20 tokens (Appendix B). The protection is real, and it belongs to
158   the prompt rather than to the budget.

187

188

189
      that a cheap decoder of the same quality exists.

191   The obvious objection is that a three-valued judge is too blunt a utility to price. It runs the
192   wrong way: bluntness caps the rate function, since Λ∗s (umax ) = − log Prps [U = umax ] for
193

      would cost an optimal policy 1.19 nats, and the decoder spends 144 times that. A utility
194
      is expensive in this sense only when its best outcome is exponentially unlikely under the
195
      safe model, which is exactly what the atom {output = x} is. Extraction and utility differ
196   because one is a rare event and the other a bounded average.

200

201

273   What the margin does not license The margin says how far apart the two rates are, not

      Conditioning on the adversary’s context is what makes s(x) the unit The claim that a

404

406   We built the family — α → 1 is the audited decoder, α → ∞ the pathwise one, and Eq. (1)

412   and the certificate with it — vacuous throughout — the four decoders span two orders of

      6   Related work
442
443   Near-access-freeness (Vyas et al., 2023) asks that a served model stay within a divergence of
444   a safe model that never saw the protected work, and CP-∆/CP-k instantiate it by combining
445
      models trained on disjoint shards (Vyas et al., 2023; Abad et al., 2025). Three results bear
      directly on ours: Cohen (2025) shows per-query access-freeness does not compose, Cohen
446
      & Zhang (2026) proves barriers for counterfactual credit attribution, and — closest of all
447
      — Segal et al. (2026) report that CP-style guarantees can be numerically uninformative
448   in realistic fine-tuning settings and demonstrate extraction against NAF-protected models.
449   We are downstream of that observation, not upstream of it. What differs is the shape of
450   the claim: they show a particular bound is loose and propose a tighter mechanism, so the
451   natural reading is that the accounting can be repaired. Proposition 1 says it cannot, for
452   every order from KL to max-divergence at once, leaving a smaller budget as the only lever
453   — which Theorem 1 then prices.
454   Our proof shape is the one used for inference-time impossibility results generally: strong wa-
455   termarking under oracle assumptions (Zhang et al., 2024), and detector accuracy bounded by
456   a total variation that goes vacuous as the distributions converge (Sadasivan et al., 2025). The
457   accounting machinery is the Rényi filter (Feldman & Zrnic, 2020) and adaptive composition
458   (Whitehouse et al., 2023; Rogers et al., 2016); the token bucket and its running maximum
459   are standard in queueing and network calculus (Loynes, 1962; Cruz, 1991; Le Boudec & Thi-
460   ran, 2001), which is where our second boundary comes from. Attacks follow the extraction
461   literature (Carlini et al., 2021; Nasr et al., 2025; Cooper et al., 2025), and methodologically
462
      we follow Aerni et al. (2024): average-case evaluation of a privacy defence hides worst-case
      failure, exactly the gap between what the budget reports and what the running maximum
463
      controls. Appendix G covers decoding-time control, memorisation lower bounds and the
464
      alignment-frontier literature.

467   7   Conclusion
468
469   A scalar divergence budget is a rate, and safety against reproducing a particular work is
470   a workload maximum; queueing theory has known those come apart since Loynes (1962),
471   and the mismatch has consequences a deployer can compute. The budget’s unit is the safe
472   model’s surprisal of the work it protects, the certificate is exactly vacuous at k = s(x) for
473   every Rényi order, and extraction begins within about a fifth of that point on every pair,
474   on either side of it. Whether any budget is both informative and useful depends on what
475   a rights-holder means by informative. No budget certifies every protected work and also
476   buys a detectable improvement: the last budget covering all of them is k = 0.583, where
477
      neither judge can tell the decoder from its own safe model. Relax every to almost every
      and a narrow window opens on the sharper judge, at k = 0.66 with 99.7% still covered, and
478
      closes again on the second, which finds nothing until k = 5.4, where none is. Theorem 1 says
479
      the obstruction is not fundamental: the decoder spends hundreds of nats for utility worth
480   hundredths. All three boundaries are computable in advance, so a deployer publishing one
481   number should publish k/s(x), and a rights-holder reading one should ask which work it
482   was computed against. Four limitations bound all of it, in full in Appendix F: one corpus of
483   sixteen English novels, so p = 3.1 × 10−5 is the smallest value it can give; an onset ratio that
484   is not constant, and interventions that move it without closing the gap between the families;
485   Theorem 1 a converse; and two judges that bracket the useful region without agreeing where
      it opens.

      Measured on the 758 reference passages under four safe models from three openly licensed
831   corpora, kcrit /s has a median of 4.25 to 6.10. That figure should not be quoted alone. The
832   running maximum in Eq. (2) is attained at the first token of the work in 87.7% to 90.5% of
833   cases and within the opening tenth in 96.7% to 97.5%, because the bucket must pay for the
834   opening out of a single step’s allowance. An adversary who supplies one token of genuine
835   prefix removes it: the same ratio falls to 1.32–1.48 and stays there when 5, 10 or 20 tokens
836   are supplied. The uncertified protection is real, and it is almost entirely a property of where
837   a generation starts.
838   The choice of denominator flatters this effect without manufacturing it, and the difference
839   is worth stating rather than asserting robustness. Repeating every measurement per token
840   leaves the ratios after a supplied prefix unchanged — 1.35–1.41 at one token and 1.30–1.45
841   at ten, against 1.32–1.48 and 1.30–1.47 per character — but lowers the whole-work median
842   to 3.88–5.98 and the fraction binding at the very first token to 82.6%–87.7%, with 93.0%–
843   95.4% inside the opening tenth. The opening effect is smaller than the per-character figures
844   alone suggest and remains the dominant term under either denominator.
845   This also explains a gap the audit measured without accounting for it. Oracle-prefix windows
846   recover 0.2301 of a passage at k = 5 against 0.0925 for a single query in an earlier audit of

848   oracle strategy is precisely one that supplies genuine prefixes, and Proposition 2 says that
849   is what dismantles the opening effect. It unifies the prefix debt with the theorem as well: δ
850   raises kcrit and the running maximum binds at the opening, so the untheorised heuristic and
851
      the theorem’s binding constraint are the same phenomenon seen twice, and an adversary

1217   More passages do not narrow the onset interval, and that is why several arms are undecided
1218   The bootstrap interval on onset / s(x) has a mean width of 0.256 across the seven pairs at

1220   with the same metric, ‘Pleias-350M’ gives a width of 0.219 at n = 100 and 0.215 at n = 458,

1224
       in Section 4: their pre-registered bands are about 0.1 wide, narrower than an interval this

1305
1306
       Access-free guarantees and their discontents Near-access-freeness (Vyas et al., 2023) asks
1307
       that a served model stay within a divergence of a safe model that never saw the protected
1308
       work, and CP-∆ and CP-k instantiate it by combining models trained on disjoint shards
1309   (Vyas et al., 2023; Abad et al., 2025). Three recent results bear directly on ours. Cohen
1310   (2025) shows that per-query access-freeness does not compose across queries; Cohen &
1311   Zhang (2026) proves barriers for counterfactual credit attribution, a sibling relaxation of
1312   differential privacy. Closest of all, Segal et al. (2026) report that CP-style guarantees can
1313   be numerically uninformative in realistic fine-tuning settings and demonstrate extraction
1314   against NAF-protected models. We are downstream of that observation, not upstream of
1315   it.
1316   What differs is the shape of the claim, and it is worth being exact about it because the two
1317   results are compatible. Their diagnosis is that memorisation puts sharp probability spikes
1318   on a few tokens, so a divergence taken over raw probabilities reads as large even where
1319   the models agree on what to emit; their repair charges relative probabilities instead. That
1320   is a change in what is metered. Proposition 1 closes a different lever, the one a deployer
1321   actually has: for every order of the same per-step charge, from KL to max-divergence, the
1322   certificate becomes vacuous at the same budget, so a stronger charge buys precision below
1323
       the threshold and never moves it. Neither result subsumes the other — ours does not say no
       repair exists, and theirs does not claim the order is the problem — but together they leave
1324
       a deployer holding a scalar budget with only one lever that moves the threshold, a smaller
1325
       budget, which Theorem 1 prices and Section 5 measures. Cohen & Zhang (2026) is the closer
1326   structural parallel, and not the one we first read it as: its non-composition is autoregressive,
1327   not across queries. Imposing counterfactual credit attribution on the next-token predictor
1328   does not make the sequence-level model satisfy it, and retrofitting credit onto a model that
1329   lacks it needs query complexity exponential in the output length. Ours is the same shape
1330   for a different quantity — a per-step charge that is honoured at every step still leaves the
1331   sequence-level event {output = x} unprotected once the budget reaches S(x) — and it is
1332   priced in nats for a single query rather than stated as a barrier.
1333
1334   Other inference-time defences, and the level none of them certifies Anchored decoding is
1335   one of several ways to intervene at serving time. CP-Fuse combines models trained on
1336   disjoint shards under a balancing property (Abad et al., 2025) and is the second mechanism
1337
       we audit; BloomScrub certifies takedowns by Bloom-filter screening with abstention (Zhang
       et al., 2025); SILO isolates legal risk in a nonparametric datastore (Min et al., 2024); others
1338
       refuse (Liu et al., 2024) or randomise the decoder (Chen et al., 2024), and for diffusion models
1339
       retrieval carries a NAF guarantee (Golatkar et al., 2024) in response to replication in image
1340   models (Somepalli et al., 2023). Alshehyari et al. (2026) benchmark output-, behavioural-
1341   and representation-level defences across two model families; the level that benchmark omits

1375   Copyright as a legal question, and which quantity it asks for Whether copyright reduces
1376   to a privacy-style definition at all is contested (Elkin-Koren et al., 2024); memorisation and
1377   infringement are distinct questions (Haviv et al., 2026; Cooper & Grimmelmann, 2025);
1378   substantial similarity has been formalised as description length with and without access to
1379   the original (Scheffler et al., 2022); and whether a model “contains a copy” has been argued
1380   to turn on how easily the work can be extracted (Lemley & Cooper, 2026), which is the
1381   quantity Section 4 locates. Where the deployer sits in the liability chain is its own literature
1382   (Henderson et al., 2023; Lee et al., 2024). We take no position on any of these; we report
1383
       what a published k does and does not tell a rights-holder who asks about one work.

1397
       imply a worst-case event bound is classical (Sason & Verdú, 2016; van Erven & Harremoës,
1398   2014), and the differential-privacy literature has developed the conversions and their costs;

1400   certificate be informative about a specific work and the mechanism remain usable. The

1403   its running maximum are standard objects in queueing and network calculus (Loynes, 1962;

1433   corpus, scale and training recipe. The memoriser reaches 0.915 near-verbatim recall on the
