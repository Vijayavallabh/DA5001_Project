\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Limitations}

\looseness=-2 \textbf{The two mechanisms are not substitutes, and the scorer binds first.} A served string is one the safe
model would have drawn, so an anchor that cannot do the task gains nothing: on TriviaQA with no
judge the meter reaches $0.618$ against selection's $0.190$ (Figure~\ref{fig:judgefree}c) --- but
only at $k=20$, where it \emph{is} the risky model, certified at $480$ nats for a $24$-token
answer; at its small budgets it is the anchor. Below that the scorer binds, gaining $3.4\times$ less than majority vote on GSM8K at
$n=64$, and at that scorer \emph{loses} F1 on CoTaEval (Appendix~\ref{app:selection}). A capability gap is the regime a
deployer most wants covered and \emph{neither} mechanism covers it.

\looseness=-1 \noindent\textbf{And it is paid for in compute, which no nat measures.} Selection needs $n=64$ for
$+0.1045$, at $61.3\times$ the meter's forward passes and a measured $35.4$--$67.2\times$ in wall-clock;
held to the meter's own compute it \emph{loses}. The \emph{gain} is the scorer's capability: a
$0.5$B scorer never reaches the meter, a $1.5$B one buys $87\%$ of the $7.6$B gain on the judged
workload and nothing on GSM8K. The \emph{price} is not --- the scorer is $9.3\%$ of the
clock --- so the bar we have not cleared is the cost of drawing and where that bar sits is open
(Appendix~\ref{app:saturation}).\looseness=-3\ Extraction is sixteen English novels plus four in French and German, judged
levels are position-dominated, and the class-level question is open: the theorem rules out a
per-token \emph{rate} and not every causal policy, and how close a policy of that shape ---
Proposition~\ref{prop:sparse}'s, now built and priced --- comes to Theorem~\ref{thm:nfl}'s frontier
is open.%
\label{sec:conclusion}
\ \textbf{In sum:} a copyright budget is not a rate. Charged per token it must grow with the work
or else be spent almost nowhere; spent once, on the draw, it is $\log n$ at any length.
