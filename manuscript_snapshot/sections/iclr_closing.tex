\section{Limitations and conclusion}\label{sec:limitations}

\textbf{The two mechanisms are not substitutes, and the scorer binds first.} $q(y) \le n\,p_s(y)$
means a served string is one the safe model would have drawn, so an anchor that cannot do the task
gains nothing at any $n$: head to head on TriviaQA with no judge the metered decoder reaches $0.618$
and selection $0.190$ --- but only at $k=20$, where it \emph{is} the risky model and its certificate
is $480$ nats for a $24$-token answer. Below that the scorer binds: a reward gains
$3.4\times$ less than majority vote on GSM8K and \emph{loses} accuracy on TriviaQA. The corpus is
sixteen English novels, judged levels are position-dominated, and how close a policy of the shape
Proposition~\ref{prop:sparse} permits comes to Theorem~\ref{thm:nfl}'s frontier is open
(Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}).

\noindent\textbf{Conclusion.}\label{sec:conclusion}
A copyright budget is not a rate. Charged per token it must grow with the work it protects or stop
changing the output; one line of the chain rule leaves no third option. Spent once, on which of $n$
draws to serve, it is $\log n$: pathwise, and constant in the work's length. $8$, not $e^{2000}$.
