\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Limitations.}

\looseness=-2 \textbf{The two mechanisms are not substitutes, and the scorer binds first.} A served string is one the safe
model would have drawn, so an anchor that cannot do the task gains nothing: on TriviaQA with no
judge the meter reaches $0.618$ against selection's $0.190$ (Figure~\ref{fig:judgefree}c) --- but
only at $k=20$, where it \emph{is} the risky model, certified at $480$ nats for a $24$-token
answer. Below that the scorer binds: it gains $3.4\times$ less than majority vote on GSM8K at $n=64$
and \emph{loses} F1 on CoTaEval, whose infringement event not even the risky model reaches (Appendix~\ref{app:selection}). A capability gap is the regime a
deployer most wants covered and \emph{neither} mechanism covers it. \textbf{The judged comparison
measures the serving configuration as much as the mechanism}: it flips under a chat template or
instruction following (Table~\ref{tab:served}), every judge is a language model, and we ran the meter at temperature $1.0$ without a penalty.
\textbf{And it is paid for in compute}: $61.3\times$ the meter's forward passes and $21.8\times$ its wall-clock in draws alone (batched, $0.22\times$ its time per request at the $70$B pair), and held
to the meter's own compute it \emph{loses}. The \emph{gain} is
the scorer's capability: a $0.5$B scorer never reaches the meter, a $1.5$B one buys $87\%$ of the
$7.6$B gain on the judged workload and nothing on GSM8K, and where that bar sits is open. The
\emph{price} is the drawing, not the scorer's one batched pass (Appendix~\ref{app:saturation}).
Nor does the certificate say much about short works: on TriviaQA answers $\log 64$ reaches $S(x)$
on $35.4\%$ of questions, against $89.6\%$ for the meter's smallest budget, so $n$ must be set
against the shortest work protected (Appendix~\ref{app:selection}). Extraction is sixteen English
novels plus four in French and German, the covert channel of Section~\ref{sec:compose}, attacked, carries $3.97$ of its $6$ bits a response, and how close a policy of the shape
Proposition~\ref{prop:sparse} permits comes to Theorem~\ref{thm:nfl}'s frontier is open.%
\label{sec:conclusion}
\ \textbf{In sum:} a copyright budget is not a rate. Charged per token it must grow with the work
or else be spent almost nowhere; spent once, on the draw, it is $\log n$ at any length.
