\section{Limitations and conclusion}\label{sec:limitations}

\textbf{The two mechanisms are not substitutes, and the scorer binds first.} $q(y) \le n\,p_s(y)$
means a served string is one the safe model would have drawn, so an anchor that cannot do the task
gains nothing at any $n$: head to head on TriviaQA with no judge the metered decoder reaches
$0.618$ and selection $0.190$ --- but only at $k=20$, where it \emph{is} the risky model and its
certificate is $480$ nats for a $24$-token answer. Below that ceiling the scorer binds: a reward
gains $3.4\times$ less than majority vote on GSM8K and \emph{loses} accuracy on TriviaQA. The
corpus is sixteen English novels and judged levels are position-dominated.

\noindent\textbf{Conclusion.}\label{sec:conclusion}
One line of the chain rule leaves a per-token budget two options: grow with the work, or be the
anchor almost everywhere. Three repairs and the one causal placement fail where it says they must.
It fixes a shape, not a value --- how close a policy of the shape Proposition~\ref{prop:sparse}
permits comes to Theorem~\ref{thm:nfl}'s frontier is open, and we measure one, not the class. Spend it once, on the draw: $8$, not $e^{2000}$.
