\section{Limitations and conclusion}\label{sec:limitations}

\textbf{The scorer binds before the anchor does.} $q(y) \le n\,p_s(y)$ means a served string is one
the safe model would have drawn, so an anchor that cannot do the task gains nothing at any $n$ ---
but a general reward gains $3.4\times$ less than majority vote on GSM8K and \emph{loses} accuracy
on TriviaQA: the scorer is the lower ceiling. Judged levels are position-dominated, the corpus is sixteen English
novels, and the zero leakage is measured against a memoriser \emph{we} fine-tuned --- it has not
been shown against a model that memorised the work in pre-training.

\paragraph{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
One line of the chain rule leaves a per-token budget two options: grow with the work, or be the
anchor almost everywhere. Three repairs and the one causal placement fail where it says they must.
It fixes a shape and not a value --- how close a policy of the shape
Proposition~\ref{prop:sparse} permits could come to Theorem~\ref{thm:nfl}'s frontier is open, and
we measure one, not the class. Spend it once, on the
draw: the promise is $8$, not $e^{2000}$.
