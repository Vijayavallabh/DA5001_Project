\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Near-access-freeness and inference-time defences}
Vyas et al.~\cite{vyas2023provable} define $k$-NAF in $\Delta_{\max}$ and KL form; Lemma~2.2 turns the former into a per-event bound, the latter needs a concentration assumption (Lemma~2.3), and $k$ must be small relative to the entropy of the work. Anchored Decoding~\cite{he2026anchored} is the first inference-time enforcement of the KL form for an arbitrary risky model, anchored to a Common Pile model~\cite{kandpal2025commonpile}. Other inference-time defences fuse shard-trained models~\cite{abad2025cpfuse}, screen with Bloom filters~\cite{zhang2025bloomscrub}, isolate risk in a datastore~\cite{min2024silo}, refuse~\cite{liu2024shield}, randomise decoding~\cite{chen2024randomization}, or retrieve with a NAF guarantee for images~\cite{golatkar2024cpr,somepalli2023diffusion}. Training-time alternatives drop tokens from the loss~\cite{hans2024goldfish}, optimise against regurgitation~\cite{chen2025parapo}, or unlearn~\cite{eldan2023whos}, and their audits~\cite{lynch2024eight,shi2025muse,lucki2025adversarial,deeb2024unlearning} found the text recoverable --- the word covers objectives demanding different evaluations~\cite{yoon2026position} and retrain equivalence is unattainable~\cite{yu2025impossibility}, part of the appeal of guaranteeing something at inference time instead. Watermarks are the other intervention certified by a divergence from the unmodified model~\cite{kirchenbauer2023watermark,kuditipudi2024distortionfree,christ2024undetectable}. Cohen~\cite{cohen2025blameless} proves NAF does not compose, which Proposition~\ref{prop:cost} quantifies here. The closest empirical precedent is VA3~\cite{li2024va3}, which amplifies a probabilistic copyright guarantee for text-to-image models through repeated interaction; Section~\ref{sec:composition} composes something different, a per-query \emph{budget} that adds by construction rather than a per-sample probability accumulating over retries, and bounds the cost. CopyShield~\cite{alshehyari2026copyshield} benchmarks output-, behavioural- and representation-level defences; the level it omits is the certified one, where low measured leakage and a vacuous certificate can coexist. These quantities feed legal questions: whether copyright reduces to a privacy-style definition~\cite{elkinkoren2024copyright}, whether memorisation is infringement~\cite{haviv2026separate,cooper2025files}, how to formalise substantial similarity~\cite{scheffler2022formalizing}, whether ``containing'' a work turns on extractability~\cite{lemley2026probabilistic}, and where the deployer sits in the liability chain~\cite{henderson2023fairuse,lee2024talkin}.

\paragraph{Guarantees enforced while decoding}
An inference-time guarantee comes in two shapes and NAF is the second. Hard-constraint methods make the output satisfy a predicate outright, by rejection sampling against a KL-controlled proposal~\cite{kim2025guaranteed}, sequential Monte Carlo~\cite{loula2025smc}, locally constrained resampling~\cite{ahmed2024resampling} or semantic conditioning~\cite{ahmed2025semantic}. Divergence methods leave the output unconstrained and bound how far the served distribution may travel from a reference; controlled decoding~\cite{mudgal2024controlled} steers a frozen model under exactly this regularisation, and best-of-$n$ is its degenerate case~\cite{gao2023scaling,beirami2025bestofn}. The distinction sets what an audit can find: a hard constraint fails visibly, on an output violating the predicate, whereas a divergence budget fails quietly, by holding at a value that bounds nothing the rights-holder asked about. How much of a work a model can hold at all is a separate axis~\cite{morris2025memorize}. The closest privacy-side siblings project a private next-token distribution onto a divergence ball around a public one~\cite{flemings2024pmixed,flemings2024adaptive,amin2024private,tang2024privacy,thareja2025dpfusion}, along the product-of-experts geodesic~\cite{hinton2002poe,liu2024dera,dekoninck2024arithmetic}.

\paragraph{Composition, per-user accounting, and auditing}
A per-query guarantee does not protect against many queries~\cite{dinur2003revealing,dwork2014algorithmic}, as multi-turn escalation defeats single-turn safety~\cite{russinovich2025crescendo}. Differential privacy answers with composition theorems, R\'enyi accounting~\cite{mironov2017renyi}, and filters and odometers~\cite{rogers2016odometers,whitehouse2023fully}, which Section~\ref{sec:odometer} borrows and Proposition~\ref{prop:sep} bounds; the banking rule is a token bucket~\cite{turner1986leaky}. The protocol follows the auditing of differential privacy by lower bounds that could contradict a claimed $\varepsilon$~\cite{jagielski2020auditing,nasr2023tight,steinke2023privacy,annamalai2024nearly,zanellabeguelin2023bayesian,pillutla2023unleashing,kong2023dpauditorium} and the adaptive evaluation of defences~\cite{athalye2018obfuscated,carlini2019evaluating,tramer2020adaptive}; the finite-precision failures of formally correct privacy mechanisms~\cite{mironov2012significance,casacuberta2022widespread} are why the accounting check runs to a numerical tolerance. Here the auditor reads the logs, as Casper et al.~\cite{casper2024blackbox} argue audits must, while the adversary only queries. An earlier audit of Anchored Decoding~\cite{vijayavallabh2026audit} produced the logs reanalysed in Section~\ref{sec:results-logs} and checked the budget with a fixed-sample empirical Bernstein bound~\cite{maurer2009empirical,mnih2008empirical}; this audit replaces it with per-trajectory invariants and anytime-valid confidence sequences~\cite{howard2021timeuniform,waudbysmith2024estimating,chugg2025closedform,chugg2023auditing,zhou2026adaptive} and adds the certificate-strength, natural-memorisation and repair audits. The cap on reproduction follows from data processing and the inversion of the binary KL divergence~\cite{polyanskiy2024information,tsybakov2009introduction,bretagnolle1979estimation}; rare-output estimation~\cite{wu2025rare} handles events too rare to observe, automated red teaming~\cite{perez2022redteaming,ganguli2022redteaming,mouret2015mapelites,samvelyan2024rainbow} searches for prompts where here the protected text fixes them, and verifiable inference~\cite{sun2024zkllm} is how a rights-holder could one day check which decoder served them.
