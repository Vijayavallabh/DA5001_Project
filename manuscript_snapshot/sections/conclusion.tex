\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
We audited what Anchored Decoding's KL budget certifies. The accounting is exact; the guarantee is an expectation bound that single outputs exceed, its per-passage consequence is vacuous at the evaluated budgets, and the protection those budgets give against a model that memorised the text holds only while the budget binds and is mostly the work of the prefix debt, which the guarantee does not describe. Each gap has a repair inside the decoder, and the protocol is released for the next such mechanism.
