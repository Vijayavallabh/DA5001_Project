\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

Near-access-freeness \citep{vyas2023provable} asks that a served model stay within a divergence of
a safe model that never saw the protected work, and CP-$\Delta$/CP-$k$ instantiate it by combining
models trained on disjoint shards \citep{vyas2023provable,abad2025cpfuse}. Three results bear
directly on ours: \citet{cohen2025blameless} shows per-query access-freeness does not compose,
\citet{cohen2026barriers} proves barriers for counterfactual credit attribution, and --- closest of
all --- \citet{segal2026provably} report that CP-style guarantees can be numerically uninformative
in realistic fine-tuning settings and demonstrate extraction against NAF-protected models. We are
downstream of that observation, not upstream of it. What differs is the shape of the claim: they
show a particular bound is loose and propose a tighter mechanism, so the natural reading is that
the accounting can be repaired. Proposition~\ref{prop:threshold} says it cannot, for every order
from KL to max-divergence at once, leaving a smaller budget as the only lever --- which
Theorem~\ref{thm:nfl} then prices.

Our proof shape is the one used for inference-time impossibility results generally: strong
watermarking under oracle assumptions \citep{zhang2024watermarks}, and detector accuracy bounded by
a total variation that goes vacuous as the distributions converge \citep{sadasivan2025can}. The
accounting machinery is the R\'enyi filter \citep{feldman2020renyifilter} and adaptive composition
\citep{whitehouse2023fully,rogers2016odometers}; the token bucket and its running maximum are
standard in queueing and network calculus
\citep{loynes1962stability,cruz1991calculus,leboudec2001network}, which is where our second
boundary comes from. Attacks follow the extraction literature
\citep{carlini2021extracting,nasr2025scalable,cooper2025extracting}, and methodologically we follow
\citet{aerni2024evaluations}: average-case evaluation of a privacy defence hides worst-case
failure, exactly the gap between what the budget reports and what the running maximum controls.
Appendix~\ref{app:related} covers decoding-time control, memorisation lower bounds and the
alignment-frontier literature.
