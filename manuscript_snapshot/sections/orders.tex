\section{The dichotomy's corollaries, tested}\label{sec:orders}

A mechanism on the vacuous horn has three degrees of freedom --- the charge, the cap, the schedule.
All three repairs were pre-registered, and the interesting part is not that they fail but
\emph{where}: each at a different place, from one measurable shared cause.

\paragraph{Repair 1: sharpen the accounting}
Proposition~\ref{prop:threshold} says a stronger divergence cannot move the threshold, so the
question is what it does to the system underneath. Eq.~\eqref{eq:renyi-path} is closed form on the
geodesic, so one bisection serves every order; four members ran the same attack and workload at the
authors' own budget $k=3$, where all four publish $K = 3T$ and the certificate is vacuous for
$100\%$ of the $758$ protected passages, and for $98.7\%$ of $9{,}870$ across a hundred books
(Appendix~\ref{app:bookmia}) --- nothing distinguishes them to a rights-holder
(Table~\ref{tab:orders}). Underneath, they span two orders of magnitude in what a compliant
adversary recovers, oracle-window recall $0.0965$ at $\alpha=1$ against $0.0012$ at $\alpha=8$, and
more than two in what they cost, $0.4\%$ of ordinary decode steps against $99.6\%$ --- one step past
$\alpha=2$ taking the decoder from serving the risky model unchanged at $97.2\%$ of steps to $9.4\%$.
Compared at \emph{matched utility} --- fidelity to $p_r$ is monotone in $k$ at fixed order, so each
order can be given the budget that buys what $\alpha=1$ buys --- the order runs from $10^{6}$ times
more dangerous to $10^{7}$ times safer over twelve pairs, and nothing measured predicts which pair a
deployer has (Appendix~\ref{app:matched}). The certificate does not
move; everything underneath it does.

\paragraph{Repair 2: lower the budget}
This is a walk onto the trivial horn, and both sides of Eq.~\eqref{eq:nfl} price the walk. Taking
$U$ to be the judge's verdict scored $1/\tfrac12/0$, the decoder spends $79$ to $171$ nats of
realised divergence to buy gains of $+0.011$ to $+0.179$ that an optimal policy could have bought for
$0.0003$ to $0.077$, a ratio of $2.8\times10^{5}$ at $k=0.5$ that never falls below $2200$
(Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}). A three-valued judge is not too blunt to price this, because bluntness
\emph{caps} the rate function: winning \emph{every} comparison would cost an optimal policy $1.30$
nats and the decoder spends $132$ times that to win a third of them. The certified and the useful
regions then fail to meet. Under two instruction-tuned judges outside the audit, $600$
comparisons per budget with order randomised, neither separates the decoder from its own safe model
at $k=0.5$ ($-1.03\sigma$, $+1.52\sigma$), where all $758$ protected passages are still covered;
both do only at $k \ge 10$ ($-6.97\sigma$, $-3.29\sigma$), where none is. Between those the judges
disagree about the crossing by nearly a decade in $k$, $0.66$ against $5.4$ --- itself a result
about how resolvable this is.

\paragraph{Repair 3: spend the budget better}
Proposition~\ref{prop:sparse} bounds this one before it is run: a schedule cannot deliver what a
bounded sequence budget does not contain, and a greedy decoder is not short of allowance but of
somewhere useful to put it. Reallocating its own realised spend
across its own trajectory is a closed form in $\psi$, computable offline and therefore a ceiling no
causal scheduler can beat \citep{tomasi2026primaldual}; it buys $3$ to $13\%$ more utility where the
certificate still says something ($k \le 1$) and under $1.4\%$ at $k=3$ (Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}).

\paragraph{What the three have in common}
The budget is not spent inefficiently on utility; it is spent \emph{efficiently on a target that is
not utility}. A metered decoder exists to move the served distribution toward $p_r$, so its budget
buys likelihood under $p_r$ and nothing else. Among $4{,}000$ anchor samples on $500$ prompts,
within-prompt on the $5{,}687$ candidate pairs a judge ranked differently, that likelihood separates
better from worse at AUC $\mathbf{0.526}$, its summed form at $0.477$ --- \emph{below} chance ---
and raw length at $0.537$. All three repairs optimise the
delivery of a currency that predicts judged quality worse than the answer's length does. A wrong
currency spent on the wrong axis is not repaired by a better meter.
