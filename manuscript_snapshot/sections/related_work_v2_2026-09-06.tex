\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

The audit draws on the definition it tests, on the memorisation and extraction results that motivate its risky models, and on four literatures that supply its vocabulary: leakage measures, the interpretation of privacy budgets, the composition of per-query guarantees, and the adaptive evaluation of certified defences.

\paragraph{Near-access-freeness and inference-time copyright defences}
Vyas et al.~\cite{vyas2023provable} define $k$-near-access-freeness ($k$-NAF): a model is $k$-NAF with respect to a work $C$ if, for every prompt, its output distribution is within $k$ of a safe model that never saw $C$, in maximum divergence ($\Delta_{\max}$) or in KL divergence. Their Lemma~2.2 turns the $\Delta_{\max}$ form into a per-event bound, $p(E \mid x) \le 2^{k} p_s(E \mid x)$, whereas the KL form yields such a bound only under a concentration assumption (their Lemma~2.3); they also observe that $k$ must be small relative to the entropy of the work for the definition to carry meaning. Anchored Decoding~\cite{he2026anchored} is the first mechanism to enforce the KL form at inference time for an arbitrary risky model, using an anchor trained on the Common Pile~\cite{kandpal2025commonpile}. Section~\ref{sec:pathwise} enforces the $\Delta_{\max}$ form with the same decoder. Related inference-time defences include CP-Fuse~\cite{abad2025cpfuse}, which fuses two models trained on disjoint shards under a balancing property; BloomScrub~\cite{zhang2025bloomscrub}, which certifies takedowns through Bloom-filter screening with abstention; SILO~\cite{min2024silo}, which isolates legal risk in a nonparametric datastore; refusal-based agents~\cite{liu2024shield}; randomised decoding~\cite{chen2024randomization}; and, for diffusion models, retrieval-augmented generation with a NAF guarantee~\cite{golatkar2024cpr} in response to replication in image models~\cite{somepalli2023diffusion}. Training-time alternatives remove memorisation before deployment, by dropping tokens from the loss~\cite{hans2024goldfish}, by preference optimisation against regurgitation~\cite{chen2025parapo}, or by unlearning~\cite{eldan2023whos}, whose evaluations~\cite{lynch2024eight,shi2025muse,lucki2025adversarial,deeb2024unlearning} found the removed text recoverable through relearning, jailbreaks, and probing; an inference-time certificate inherits that evaluation standard. Watermarks are the other inference-time intervention whose guarantee is a divergence from the unmodified model~\cite{kirchenbauer2023watermark,kuditipudi2024distortionfree,christ2024undetectable}, with distortion-freeness the case of zero divergence. On the theory side, Cohen~\cite{cohen2025blameless} proves that NAF is tainted by idea-derived prompts and does not compose across queries; Proposition~\ref{prop:cost} gives the quantitative form of the second result for this decoder. Elkin-Koren et al.~\cite{elkinkoren2024copyright} question whether copyright reduces to a privacy-style definition at all, Haviv et al.~\cite{haviv2026separate} and Cooper and Grimmelmann~\cite{cooper2025files} separate memorisation from infringement, Scheffler et al.~\cite{scheffler2022formalizing} formalise substantial similarity as description length with and without access to the original, and Lemley and Cooper~\cite{lemley2026probabilistic} argue that whether a model ``contains a copy'' turns on how easily the work can be extracted, which is the quantity Proposition~\ref{prop:cost} bounds. Henderson et al.~\cite{henderson2023fairuse} and Lee et al.~\cite{lee2024talkin} locate the deployer in the liability chain.

\paragraph{Memorisation and extraction}
Language models reproduce training text verbatim~\cite{carlini2021extracting}, at rates that grow with model size, duplication, and prompt length~\cite{carlini2023quantifying,huang2024demystifying}, and even under benign prompts~\cite{aerni2025nonadversarial}; counterfactual memorisation~\cite{zhang2023counterfactual} separates the rare-document memorisation that copyright concerns from the reproduction of common text, which is the distinction Section~\ref{sec:leakage} draws for the anchor. Extraction succeeds against production systems through divergence prompts~\cite{nasr2025scalable}, instruction-style prompting~\cite{kassem2025alpaca}, and best-of-$n$ sampling~\cite{ahmed2026extracting}, whose success grows as a power law in the number of attempts~\cite{hughes2024bestofn}. Cooper et al.~\cite{cooper2025extracting} show that Llama~3.1~70B reproduces most of \emph{Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone} from its opening words, which is why Section~\ref{sec:natural} audits the mechanism with that model on that book; Hayes et al.~\cite{hayes2025measuring} show that extraction probability depends on the decoding scheme, which is why both temperature-1 and the authors' decoding settings are reported. Ippolito et al.~\cite{ippolito2023preventing} show that verbatim filters are evaded by trivial format changes. Benchmarks such as CopyBench~\cite{chen2024copybench}, whose book split we use, CoTaEval~\cite{wei2024cotaeval}, and Copyright-Bench~\cite{hui2026copyrightbench} measure literal and non-literal reproduction with the metrics adopted here: ROUGE~\cite{lin2004rouge}, longest and accumulated common substrings, MinHash similarity~\cite{broder1997resemblance}, and near-verbatim recall~\cite{ahmed2026extracting}. The passages behind CopyBench were introduced for membership inference~\cite{shi2024detecting}; Duan et al.~\cite{duan2024membership} and Ertan et al.~\cite{ertan2026evidentiary} caution against reading membership signals as evidence, and copyright traps~\cite{meeus2024traps} and DE-COP~\cite{duarte2024decop} address document-level detection, which is why held-out passages are treated here as a prompt set rather than as evidence about training data. Schwarzschild et al.~\cite{schwarzschild2024rethinking}, Karamolegkou et al.~\cite{karamolegkou2023copyright}, Freeman et al.~\cite{freeman2024exploring}, and Panaitescu-Liess et al.~\cite{panaitescu2025watermarking} study memorisation of copyrighted text from compression, legal, frontier-model, and watermarking perspectives.

\paragraph{What a divergence budget measures}
Quantitative information flow long ago separated average leakage from the probability of a one-try guess: Shannon-entropy leakage does not bound it, min-entropy leakage does~\cite{smith2009foundations}, gain functions model adversaries who profit from partial guesses~\cite{alvim2012measuring}, maximal leakage is the multiplicative increase of any guessing probability~\cite{issa2020operational}, and pointwise maximal leakage measures the leakage of a single realised outcome~\cite{saeidian2023pointwise}. In these terms KL-NAF bounds average leakage, $\Delta_{\max}$-NAF bounds maximal leakage, the rights-holder's event is a one-try guess of a substantial part of the work, and the realised log-likelihood ratio of Section~\ref{sec:tails} is the pointwise quantity. The same distinction underlies the reconstruction bounds derived from R\'enyi and hypothesis-testing privacy~\cite{guo2022bounding,balle2022reconstructing,hayes2023bounding,kaissis2023bounding}, of which Eq.~\eqref{eq:cap} is the NAF analogue, and the observation that memorisation and extractability are governed by different constants~\cite{che2026memorization}. That a certificate can be formally valid and practically vacuous also has precedents: certified radii of randomised smoothing~\cite{cohen2019certified} shrink with dimension~\cite{kumar2020curse,blum2020random}, and the $\varepsilon$ values of deployed differential privacy are often too large for the formal guarantee to bind while empirical attacks still succeed~\cite{jayaraman2019evaluating}. How to choose and interpret a privacy budget is its own literature~\cite{lee2011howmuch,hsu2014economic,brown2022privacy,cyffers2025setting}; the epsilon registry of Dwork, Kohli, and Mulligan~\cite{dwork2019expose} is the model for the certificate card of Section~\ref{sec:discussion}. Why the mean is the wrong summary of a realised log-ratio is classical: information density concentrates only for long sequences~\cite{cover2006elements}, finite-length behaviour is governed by the varentropy~\cite{kontoyiannis2014optimal}, about $e^{D_{\mathrm{KL}}}$ samples are needed before importance sampling from one distribution reaches another~\cite{chatterjee2018sample}, and the KL divergence of best-of-$n$ selection is $\log n - (n-1)/n$~\cite{gao2023scaling,beirami2025bestofn}, which is the reading of $K$ nats as best-of-$e^{K}$ selection used in Section~\ref{sec:certificate}. Proposition~\ref{prop:conc} rests on Freedman's martingale inequality~\cite{freedman1975tail,tropp2011freedman} and its time-uniform descendants~\cite{howard2021timeuniform}.

\paragraph{Composition and per-user accounting}
That a per-query guarantee does not protect against many queries is the fundamental law of information recovery~\cite{dinur2003revealing,dwork2014algorithmic}, and multi-turn escalation defeats single-turn safety in the same way~\cite{russinovich2025crescendo}. Differential privacy answers it with composition theorems, R\'enyi accounting~\cite{mironov2017renyi}, and, when parameters are chosen adaptively, privacy filters and odometers~\cite{rogers2016odometers,whitehouse2023fully}, whose martingale analysis is the one Section~\ref{sec:odometer} borrows for a per-user KL budget. The banking rule of the mechanism is, in networking terms, a token bucket of unbounded depth~\cite{turner1986leaky}, and the bank cap of Section~\ref{sec:odometer} is the depth limit that bounds bursts. The closest privacy-side siblings of anchored decoding are private next-token prediction methods that project a private model's distribution onto a divergence ball around a public model's, per token, with a budget over the sequence~\cite{flemings2024pmixed,flemings2024adaptive,amin2024private,tang2024privacy,thareja2025dpfusion}; the geodesic they and He et al.\ traverse is a product of experts~\cite{hinton2002poe}, the same family that decoding-time realignment~\cite{liu2024dera} and model arithmetic~\cite{dekoninck2024arithmetic} use to interpolate between models at inference time.

\paragraph{Auditing methodology}
The protocol follows the auditing of differential privacy, where a claimed $\varepsilon$ is tested by lower bounds that could contradict it~\cite{jagielski2020auditing,nasr2023tight,steinke2023privacy,annamalai2024nearly,zanellabeguelin2023bayesian,pillutla2023unleashing,kong2023dpauditorium}, and the adaptive evaluation of certified and empirical defences, in which a defence is judged by attacks designed against it~\cite{athalye2018obfuscated,carlini2019evaluating,tramer2020adaptive}; implementation audits of privacy libraries, where finite-precision arithmetic broke formally correct mechanisms~\cite{mironov2012significance,casacuberta2022widespread}, are the reason the accounting check of Section~\ref{sec:accounting} is run to a numerical tolerance rather than assumed. Casper et al.~\cite{casper2024blackbox} argue that rigorous audits need more than black-box access; here the audit reads the decoder's logs while the adversary uses only queries. An earlier audit of Anchored Decoding~\cite{vijayavallabh2026audit} produced the logs reanalysed in Section~\ref{sec:results-logs} and checked the sequence-level budget with a fixed-sample empirical Bernstein bound on sampled spends; the present audit replaces that bound with the per-trajectory invariant and anytime-valid confidence sequences~\cite{howard2021timeuniform,waudbysmith2024estimating,chugg2025closedform,chugg2023auditing,zhou2026adaptive}, for which a fixed-sample bound~\cite{maurer2009empirical,mnih2008empirical} lacks validity under optional stopping, and adds the certificate-strength, natural-memorisation, and repair audits. The cap on the reproduction probability follows from the data-processing inequality and the inversion of the binary KL divergence~\cite{polyanskiy2024information,tsybakov2009introduction,bretagnolle1979estimation}; rare-output estimation~\cite{wu2025rare} is the tool for the events too rare to observe directly. Automated red teaming~\cite{perez2022redteaming,ganguli2022redteaming} and quality-diversity search~\cite{mouret2015mapelites,samvelyan2024rainbow} search for adversarial prompts; here the queries are fixed by the protected text itself, which is the composition setting, rather than searched. Verifiable inference~\cite{sun2024zkllm} is how a rights-holder could one day check that the certified decoder was the one that served them.
