\paragraph{The residual fraction belongs to the evaluation, not to the models}
The fraction is neither fixed nor noise: it falls, monotonically in rank, with the number of
\emph{words} the evaluation hands the adversary --- $1.166$ and $1.053$ at $7.5$ words, $1.027$ and
$0.993$ at $9.5$, and $0.878$--$0.926$ at $13.9$--$15.0$ --- at Spearman $-0.958$, exact
$p = 0.0002$, and that ordering was fitted to seven of the nine points, with the eighth
\emph{predicted} before its anchor was fine-tuned ($1.048$ against $1.027$). Memorisation strength and
vocabulary size both fail to explain it, and because a twenty-token seed is exactly twenty times the
characters per token the ranking is confounded with granularity, so we intervened. Nine arms, each
pre-registered with its refuting band, each moving \texttt{--seed-tokens} alone: giving Pleias-1.2B
the KL3M seed --- $6.9$ words instead of $14.6$ --- moves it from $0.866$ $[0.72, 0.95]$ to $1.004$
$[0.99, 1.23]$, intervals that do not overlap, and the reverse swap moves both KL3M pairs back down.
Handing all nine the same $13.6$--$15.0$ \emph{words} rather than twenty tokens drops the spread in
onset$/s(x)$ from $0.289$ to $0.113$, every pair that moved moving \emph{down}: $61\%$ of what reads
as a property of the anchors is the benchmark's fixed-token seed, and $39\%$ is not. It acts on the
budget rather than the memoriser --- $s(x)$ moves under $1.6\%$ across those arms while
$k_{\mathrm{crit}}$, computed from the anchor alone, moves $7$ to $20\%$ and the onset follows to a
mean error of $5.1\%$ against a no-change null's $11.0\%$ --- so a longer prefix does not make the
memoriser better at the work, it
starts the target at a cheaper point on its own surprisal profile. Nor is this a quirk of our
harness: extractability is defined ``with $k$ tokens of context'' \citep{carlini2023quantifying}, so
two measurements under different tokenizers at one token budget differ in a quantity that drives what
they measure. Appendix~\ref{app:seed} gives all nine arms and their bands.

\paragraph{Two refinements we derived, pre-registered, and had to reject}
The decoding geometry supplies a candidate of its own. On the geometric path $\psi_t(1) = 0$
identically, so at full tilt the per-token charge along a target is the mean
$D_{\mathrm{KL}}(p_{r,t}\,\|\,p_{s,t})$ on that target's own prefixes, which for a risky model
near-deterministic on a memorised $x$ collapses to
\begin{equation}
r(x) \;=\; s_s(x) - s_r(x),
\label{eq:req}
\end{equation}
the rate a decoder must permit before it can pay for $x$ at all, predicting an onset ratio of
$1 - s_r/s_s$ that must \emph{move} with memorisation. Committed for the last five pairs before any
was swept, under three rules, it is refuted by all three: every miss low, held-out errors of $0.352$,
$0.251$ and $0.481$ nats, and a directional claim that inverts (Appendix~\ref{app:collapse}).
Eq.~\eqref{eq:req} is a correct statement about what a decoder must \emph{afford} and a wrong one
about where leakage \emph{begins}: the budget granting access to a \emph{class} of works is not the
budget at which one work becomes recoverable, and no scalar rate is both. The second refinement fails
in the complementary direction. Warping the anchor's logits to $\tau = 0.4$ moves $s(x)$ itself by
$67\%$ and $62\%$ on two pairs with everything else fixed, and the onset follows at an elasticity of
$+0.72$ $[+0.41, +0.84]$ and $+0.61$ $[+0.40, +0.96]$ --- refuting a budget in constant nats twice
over, without reaching exact proportionality. There $k_{\mathrm{crit}}$ misses by $21.9\%$ against a
no-change null's $12.3\%$ and ranks the nine pairs at $\rho = +0.036$: it tracks which token of a
fixed profile must be generated first, not a rescaling of the profile. The two propositions bound
different things, and only one is a unit.
