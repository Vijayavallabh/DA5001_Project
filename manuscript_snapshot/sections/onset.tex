\section{Where extraction actually begins}\label{sec:onset}

Proposition~\ref{prop:threshold} locates the budget at which the certificate stops saying anything.
It does not say leakage begins there; if leakage began far above, the threshold would be a
curiosity. It does not. We measure the onset --- the budget at which mean
single-query recall first reaches $0.01$, interpolated inside its bracket on a grid of at least
twelve points, with $k=-1$ and $k=0$ baselines on the same prompts and seeds --- on nine pairs with
nine distinct anchors and six tokenizers. The first is the audited pair; in the other eight
memorisation is the only difference --- we fine-tune a model until it reproduces the passages and
fuse it against the \emph{unmodified} model it came from --- and a pair enters only on
\emph{sampled} $k=-1$ recall above $0.10$ (Appendix~\ref{app:second-anchor}). Over a
$1.61\times$ span in $s(x)$ per token and $1.87\times$ per character, \textbf{every onset falls
between $0.88$ and $1.17$ of $s(x)$}, and for the two KL3M
pairs the interval excludes $1$ from below --- there the certificate expires before the danger
starts. The threshold is therefore not bookkeeping but, to within a fifth, where a compliant
adversary begins recovering protected text (Table~\ref{tab:onset}).

The residual fifth is not a property of the models but of the evaluation: it tracks the number of
\emph{words} a fixed-token seed hands the adversary, at Spearman $-0.958$ (exact $p = 0.0002$), and
handing all nine the same $13.6$--$15.0$ words rather than twenty tokens drops the spread in
onset$/s(x)$ from $0.289$ to $0.113$, every pair that moved moving \emph{down}: $61\%$ of it is the
benchmark's seed convention, a caution for anyone measuring extraction under a seed fixed in tokens
\citep{carlini2023quantifying}. Two
refinements we derived and pre-registered, $r(x) = s_s(x) - s_r(x)$ and $k_{\mathrm{crit}}$, were
refuted in complementary directions, while warping the anchor moves the onset with it,
refuting a budget quoted in constant nats.
Appendix~\ref{app:onset} has all of it, bands committed first.
