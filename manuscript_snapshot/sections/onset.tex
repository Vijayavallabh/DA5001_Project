\paragraph{Where extraction actually begins}\label{sec:onset}

Proposition~\ref{prop:threshold} locates the budget at which the certificate stops saying anything;
if leakage began far above it, that would be bookkeeping. It does not. On nine pairs with nine
distinct anchors and six tokenizers, \textbf{every onset falls between $0.88$ and $1.17$ of $s(x)$},
and for the two KL3M pairs the interval excludes $1$ from below (Table~\ref{tab:onset}). Nine pairs
over a $1.6\times$ span will not carry a law and we claim none; what they show is that the threshold
is not bookkeeping. The residual fifth is the evaluation's, tracking the \emph{words} a fixed-token
seed hands the adversary ($\rho = -0.958$) --- a caution for anyone measuring extraction under a
seed fixed in tokens \citep{carlini2023quantifying}.
