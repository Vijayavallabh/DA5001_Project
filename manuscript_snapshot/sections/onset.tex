\paragraph{Where extraction actually begins}\label{sec:onset}

Proposition~\ref{prop:threshold} locates the budget at which the certificate stops saying anything;
if leakage began far above it, that would be bookkeeping. It does not. On nine pairs with nine
distinct anchors and six tokenizers, over a $1.61\times$ span in $s(x)$, \textbf{every onset falls
between $0.88$ and $1.17$ of $s(x)$}, and for the two KL3M pairs the interval excludes $1$ from
below --- there the certificate expires before the danger starts (Table~\ref{tab:onset}). The
residual fifth belongs to the evaluation, not the models: it tracks the \emph{words} a fixed-token
seed hands the adversary at Spearman $-0.958$ (exact $p = 0.0002$), and equalising them removes
$61\%$ of the spread --- a caution for anyone measuring extraction under a seed fixed in tokens
\citep{carlini2023quantifying}. Appendix~\ref{app:onset} has the protocol, the table, the two
refuted refinements and the bands, committed first.
