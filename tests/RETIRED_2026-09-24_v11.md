Section 3, restated Propositions 2 and 4, replaced the 400-nat response counts in Section 2 and
Table 1 with the query horizon and the near-verbatim sampling floor, and added the empty-answer, windowed-meter
and prefix-extension results. For space it moved three floats to the appendix: Figure `fig:breadth`

- `n = 256` in the abstract and the introduction
- "no better than the answer's length"
- "under 1.4% at k=3"
- the meter's own k=10 gain
- "the gains over each arm's own control"

broken once, the guard named was confirmed to fail, and every mutation asserted it landed exactly once.
